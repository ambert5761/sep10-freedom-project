# Entry 1: Fashion and designing 
##### 10/27/26

## Why did I choose fashion?
#### The topic I choose for my year long project was fashion and designing. I enjoy making new types of fashion trends out of clothes I don't wear anymore. For example, I turn my old T-shirts into tank tops and jeans into shorts. I am obsessed with different fashion trends on social media and would try getting my own fabric so I can join in on the trends. I walk into different thrift stores around my neighborhood and get a feel of happiness, seeing all these different patterns and design on clothes gives me a more open idea on how to style my outfits. I like showing off my visions through designing and it has been a big part of why I chose this topic. 

The two main link that expressed my thoughts on fashion is https://www.insidefashiondesign.com/post/beyond-the-runway-30-reasons-i-love-fashion-design. This link had inspired me to see how beautiful the fashion industry is, I get to put all my ideas in one billboard and in the future I want to be famous enough so I can show my work and travel. The other link is https://www.vogue.com/tag/misc/victorias-secret-fashion-show. Vogue and Victora Secret have been a big inspiration for me since I was a little kid. I always saw Vogue magazines and wish that would be me one day, I see the VS show and hope my designs would be on stage one day, Vogue and VS really shows off a big fashion industry and it would be a pleasure if it was me.

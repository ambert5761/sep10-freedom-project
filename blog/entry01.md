# Entry 1: Fashion and designing 
##### 10/27/26

## Why did I choose fashion?
#### The topic I choose for my year long project was fashion and designing. I enjoy making new types of fashion trends out of clothes I don't wear anymore. For example, I turn my old T-shirts into tank tops and jeans into shorts. I am obsessed with different fashion trends on social media and would try to get my own fabric so I can join in on the trend. I walked into different thrift stores around my neighborhood and get a feel of happiness, seeing all these different patterns and design on clothes gives me an more open idea on how to style my outfits. I like showing off my visions through designing and it has been a big part of why I chose this topic. 

The two main link that expressed my thoughts on fashion is https://www.insidefashiondesign.com/post/beyond-the-runway-30-reasons-i-love-fashion-design

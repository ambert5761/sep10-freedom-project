## Overview/Introduction/Context
* Fashion and designing is a way to express yourself creatively. In fashion and designing you are able to create your own designs based on your style, for example many people cut their old t-shirts into crop tops and jeans into shorts. But above all you get to create your own things, a lot of people use apps to create 3D models of their work which is what i love about this topic.  
* Some challenges you can face while doing fashion and designing is having to spend long hours working on your designs, sometime when i want to create something new at home it takes me hours to make my idea come to life. What may also be challenging is coming up with new ideas, as you get more into designing.
